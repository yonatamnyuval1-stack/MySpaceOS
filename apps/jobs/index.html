About Jobs

White-series OS compute runtime, not a branded logo series.

What it is

Jobs is the safety valve for heavy work. Everyday opens and short commands often run directly; this queue absorbs overflow with four pools: Interactive, Connect, Shell, and Background.

Pools

  • Interactive : UI-facing launches
  • Connect : mail, messaging, browsers
  • Shell : commands and scripts
  • Background : low priority / budgeted
