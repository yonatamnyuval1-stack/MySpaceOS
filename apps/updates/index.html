About

How My Space updates work

Updates is a platform service for My Space — not a desktop tile. It shows the build you are running now, lists major releases when they are ready, and keeps a full changelog of past notes.

Current build

The home page opens on your installed version: My Space logo, version number, and the release notes that match that build from the catalog. If there is no exact catalog row for your version, Notes may reference the closest earlier release so you still see what shipped.

New updates

When a major entry is added to the catalog and is newer than your installed version, it appears under New updates on the same page. Apply marks it and relaunches My Space so the new build is active. Skip hides it from that list; it remains visible in History. New releases surface here — not in the notification bell.

History

History is the full changelog: every release in the catalog, newest first. Filter by applied, pending, or skipped, or search by version and notes. Use it when you want the long trail of what changed across builds.

Check & catalog

Check now re-reads the catalog and refreshes pending counts. Release notes live in config/updates.json. Shell shortcuts: updates(open), updates(status), updates(history), updates(check).

At a glance

  • Current — installed version, name, and explanation
  • New updates — major releases ready to apply
  • Apply — save + relaunch My Space
  • Skip — hide from new updates (still in History)
  • History — full catalog changelog
  • Check now — refresh the catalog for pending releases
