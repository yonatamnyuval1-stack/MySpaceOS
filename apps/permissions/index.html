Decide what Mind, Jobs, Bridge, and external tools may do on this PC.

Overview

A single place for platform access: tools, alerts, compute, devices, and external APIs.

AI tools

Enable or disable tools the Mind side assistant can call. Mutating tools ask for confirmation when policy requires it.

Notifications

Mail alerts and the sender blocklist used by the taskbar bell.

Jobs

What the OS compute runtime is allowed to queue: shell, scripts, launches, and focus behavior.

Bridge

Phones and devices trusted to exchange files and clipboard with this PC.

No trusted devices

Pair a phone from Platform → OS Bridge.

External

Composio / Pulse external toolkits: master switch and allowlist.

About Permissions

White-series platform service: access control for My Space.

What it is

Permissions centralizes gates that already exist across Mind, Jobs, Notifications, Bridge, and Pulse external, so you do not hunt through five panels to revoke access.

What it is not

It does not replace Vault unlock, MSL capability minting, or Flow's per-run approve bar. Those stay on their own surfaces; this app points at the lasting prefs behind them.
