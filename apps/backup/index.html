About

How My Space backups work

Backup is a platform service for saving and restoring My Space user data. It exports a zip you choose, and restore replaces local data after a restart — not a desktop tile.

What is included

The zip contains the Electron userData folder (app settings, notes, chat history, Mail accounts metadata, Jobs state, and other local JSON). In development it may also include the desktop user-config.json when that file lives outside userData.

Export

Choose a destination zip. My Space builds a snapshot, compresses it, and records the path in History. Keep copies on another drive or cloud folder you control.

Restore

Pick a previous zip. The archive is staged, then My Space relaunches and applies it before other services write. Close other My Space windows first. This overwrites current local data with the backup.
